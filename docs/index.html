Your script, at the speed of your voice.

TalkPrompter is a free Windows app that listens while you read and scrolls the script for you. No fixed speed. No chasing the text.

Needs the free .NET 8 Desktop Runtime. Windows offers to install it if missing.

Why it is different

A prompter that actually listens

Classic teleprompters scroll at a fixed speed and make you chase the text. This one keeps up with you.

Follows your voice

No fixed scroll speed to chase. The script moves exactly as fast as you speak, and stops the moment you stop.

Handles retakes

Messed up a sentence? Just read it again from anywhere. The prompter finds you and follows you back.

100% private

Speech recognition runs on your PC. No cloud, no account, no subscription. Your voice never leaves your machine.

Word and text files

Drag a .docx or .txt onto the window. Keep editing in Word and the prompter reloads on every save.

Camera mode

One click turns the app into a narrow strip right under your webcam, so viewers cannot see your eyes move.

Made for recording

Script library with per script resume, mirror mode for glass rigs, global hotkeys, countdown, and time left while you read.

Get started

Up and running in a minute

No account, no setup wizard maze. Three steps and you are reading.

Install

Download and run the setup. It is small and keeps itself up to date.

Get the voice pack

On first start, pick the language you read in and the app downloads its voice pack once. 12 languages, from English and German to Polish and Indonesian.

Read

Paste your script or drop a file on the window, press Start, and just read.

Ready when you are

Free, open source, and yours to keep. No strings attached.
